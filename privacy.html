Privacy Policy

Mahjong School · Last updated October 13, 2026

Mahjong School is a learning app for American Mahjong. We built it so it doesn't need to know anything about you.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It doesn't send your information anywhere.

What stays on your device

Your lesson progress, quiz scores, tile style and which practice questions you've already seen are saved only on your own device, so the app can remember where you left off. You can erase them any time with "Reset my progress" at the bottom of the home screen, or by deleting the app.

Photos

The Hand helper lets you take or pick a photo of your rack. The photo is only shown on your screen while you use it. It is never uploaded, shared or saved by the app.

Internet use

The app works offline. It only uses the internet to download the app itself and its updates.

Children

The app doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
